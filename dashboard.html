    Help and FAQ

    1. How to Add a Transaction?

    Navigate to the Add Transaction tab, fill out the form with the transaction details, and click Add Transaction.

    2. How to View My Current Balance?

    Click on the View Balance tab to see your current financial balance, calculated from your income and expenses.

    3. How to Generate a Financial Summary?

    Go to the Generate Summary tab, select the desired period (Weekly or Monthly), and view your financial summary, including total income, expenses, and category breakdowns.

    4. How to Export My Transactions?

    In the Transactions tab, use the Export as PDF or Export as CSV buttons at the bottom to download your transactions for offline use or sharing.

    5. How to Use the Chatbot?

    On the left, you will find the Financial Assistant section. Type your financial questions or requests in the input field and click the send button to receive assistance.

    6. How to Logout?

    Click the Logout button in the navigation bar to securely sign out of your account.
